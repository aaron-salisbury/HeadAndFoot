# Head and Foot

Head and Foot is a small, 1v1, implementation of the like-named card game. Inspired by classic Windows Solitaire.

It is a Windows Forms side project built with a deliberately straightforward desktop experience, whose intention is to have the look and feel of the classic Solitaire; as if they could have been shipped together.

## Playing

You play one-on-one against the computer over four rounds, following the
[Hand and Foot rules from Official Game Rules](https://officialgamerules.org/game-rules/hand-and-foot/)
for two players: five decks with jokers, an 11-card hand and an 11-card foot each.

- **Draw:** click the stock to draw two cards, or click the discard pile to pick it up.
- **Meld:** click cards in your hand to select them, then click your meld area (above your hand) to start a meld, or click one of your melds to add to it. Right-click or press Esc to clear the selection.
- **Discard:** select one card and click the discard pile, or double-click the card.
- **Game > Deal** (F2) starts a new game; **Help > How to Play** (F1) summarises the rules.

## Rule details

Where the published rules leave room for interpretation, the game does the following:

- A meld is closed once it becomes a seven-card book; a new meld of that rank may then be started.
- Melds laid down before opening are outlined on the table until their combined printed value reaches the round's minimum (50, 90, 120, 150). Click an outlined meld to take it back; any still outlined when you discard return to your hand.
- Picking up the discard pile melds the top card with two matching naturals from your hand, joining your unfinished meld of that rank if you have one. Before opening, the rest of the pile is held aside until the opening is complete; drawing from the stock instead puts the pile back.
- The pile cannot be picked up while a three or wild card is on top.

Five house rules replace the published ones:

- Picking up the discard pile takes only the top card and the two beneath it, rather than the top seven. The table fans those three cards so they can be seen before deciding.
- The published rule is one deck more than the number of players, but at least five decks are used. Three decks leave a one-on-one game too few cards: the stock runs dry before either player can build their books without a partner.
- When the stock runs out, every discard except the top card is shuffled into a new stock and play continues, so a round usually lasts until someone goes out. If the stock and the discards under the top card together cannot supply a full two-card draw, the round ends with no going-out bonus. This keeps a round from running forever once nearly everything is melded and only unmeldable cards, such as threes, are left circulating.
- Red threes are not laid out. They are held and discarded like any other card (a three on top blocks the pile), and each one still in a player's hand or foot when the round ends costs 500 points.
- A player goes out by melding their last card, never by discarding it. Once playing from the foot, a player must either meld every card or keep at least two, so they can discard one and still hold the other.

## Building

The application targets **.NET Framework 2.0** so it can run on Windows 98 with the
.NET Framework 2.0 redistributable. Its `app.config` also allows the 4.x runtime, so it
runs on current Windows without enabling .NET 3.5. Modern C# syntax is used where it
compiles against the net20 reference assemblies.

```powershell
msbuild src/HeadAndFoot.Tests/HeadAndFoot.Tests.csproj /t:restore /p:RestorePackagesConfig=true /p:SolutionDir=src\
msbuild src/HeadAndFoot.slnx /p:Configuration=Release
vstest.console src/HeadAndFoot.Tests/bin/Release/HeadAndFoot.Tests.dll
```

The executable is `src/HeadAndFoot.DesktopApp/bin/Release/HeadAndFoot.exe`; its assets are embedded.

## Layout

| Project | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `HeadAndFoot.Business` | Cards, meld rules, the game engine, scoring, and the computer opponent (net20, no UI). |
| `HeadAndFoot.DesktopApp` | The WinForms table, menus, and dialogs (net20). |
| `HeadAndFoot.Tests` | MSTest unit tests, including complete computer-versus-computer games. |

## Card art

The card faces, jokers, and back are original and generated by
[`tools/CardArt/New-CardArt.ps1`](tools/CardArt/New-CardArt.ps1), which draws them with
GDI+ into `src/HeadAndFoot.DesktopApp/Assets/Cards/cards.png` at the classic 71 x 96
card size. Rerun the script after changing it. See
[`Attributions.txt`](src/HeadAndFoot.DesktopApp/Assets/Attributions.txt) for credits.
