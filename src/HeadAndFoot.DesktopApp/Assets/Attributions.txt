Playing card art (faces, jokers, and back) is original to Head and Foot. It is drawn by tools/CardArt/New-CardArt.ps1 in the project repository, in the spirit of classic desktop card games, without copying any existing card artwork.

Application icons are original artwork by Aaron Salisbury.

Game rules follow "Hand and Foot" as published by Official Game Rules:
https://officialgamerules.org/game-rules/hand-and-foot/
